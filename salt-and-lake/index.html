What you're hearing

B4 sounds at 493.9 Hz and C5 at 523.3 Hz. They are a minor second apart, and their difference of about 29 Hz is heard as a rough, buzzing beat. That roughness is the clash.

Over C, E and G, the B becomes the major seventh of the chord and the top C doubles the root. Nothing about the two upper strings changes. The ear now hears them as colour inside C major seventh.

The salt and the lake

A spoonful of salt stirred into a glass of water makes it undrinkable. Stir the same spoonful into a lake and you can't taste it. The salt is the same in both. What changed is the size of what holds it.
