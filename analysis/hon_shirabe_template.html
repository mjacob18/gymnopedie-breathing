How the timing was found

Honkyoku has no beat to count. The player blows one long phrase, lets the note fade, breathes in, and starts the next phrase with a sharp attack. Those attacks stand out in the recording's loudness, which gives 17 phrases from 0:00 to the last fade at 4:10.

Each breath begins on an attack. You breathe out with the player, then breathe in while the note fades and the player rests.

Why the breaths vary

The phrases run from 9.3 s to 25.7 s, 14.7 s on average. By default a phrase longer than 15 s is split into two equal breaths, so breaths stay between 8 and 15 s, about 5.5 per minute on average.
