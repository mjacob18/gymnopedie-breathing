How the timing was found

Bass onsets were detected in the recording and fitted to a bar grid. That gave 86 bars for No. 1, with the left hand alternating G and D each bar, then 69 bars for No. 3. A breath begins every fourth bar, so it follows the pianist's slowing at phrase ends.

No. 3 has a few longer phrases. There the breath spans six bars instead of four.

Why it is not exactly 6 per minute

This pianist averages 87 beats per minute, so one bar lasts 2.07 s. A four-bar breath averages 8.8 s, about 6.8 breaths per minute, ranging from 7.2 to 12.9 s.
